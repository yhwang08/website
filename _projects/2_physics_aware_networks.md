title: Physics-aware neural networks for hydrology
description: Embedding mass conservation into neural networks to study how physical constraints shape performance, generalization, and learned representations.
importance: 2
category: research
---

Purely data-driven models can be highly accurate but are not guaranteed to respect basic physical laws. This work develops physics-aware neural networks that embed **mass-conservation principles** directly into the model, and investigates how these physical constraints affect:

- **Predictive performance**
- **Generalization** across diverse hydroclimatic conditions
- **Learned representations** inside the network
